# Code of Conduct

LeslieTavern welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Project spaces should remain useful, respectful, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code on their merits.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and assume good faith.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and content boundaries.
- Accept corrections and help newcomers understand project conventions.
- Keep technical disagreements focused on the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private chats, identity, credentials, or contact details.
- Sexualized or otherwise inappropriate conduct directed at contributors.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, impersonation, or repeated bad-faith demands.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled with discretion and without retaliation.
